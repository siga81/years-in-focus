# Changelog

This changelog covers user-visible Years in Focus releases. Internal
development builds are not listed as releases.

## 0.1.4 – 16 August 2026

- Refined the storyboard interface with more consistent sections, compact
  controls and improved scrolling in smaller windows.
- Added manual eye correction from cards and the image preview, including a
  centered, zoomable detail view. Corrections are saved only in the respective
  project after explicit confirmation.
- Made quality selection and maximum side-view assessment consistent; the target
  image count now refers directly to the images currently matching the filter.
- Added time-lapse mode with frames per image, crossfade, yearly limit and an
  optional frontal-image filter.
- Introduced the `.yif.json` project format.
- Renamed Windows executables to `YearsInFocus.exe` and `YearsInFocusCLI.exe`.
- Added a revised quick guide, a structured Images menu and a Ko-fi link in the
  About dialog.

## 0.1.3 – 12 August 2026

- Made previews consistently use the selected output aspect ratio, including
  portrait and square formats.
- Improved the persistent movie-duration estimate.
- Added quality-aware automatic selection and bundled an LGPLv3 FFmpeg build for
  video export and optional background music.

## 0.1.2 – 12 August 2026

- Introduced the tabbed work area for general settings, storyboard, movie,
  audio/slides and export.
- Added output presets, optional opening/closing slides, background music and
  project-based relinking of moved source images.

## 0.1.1 – 9 August 2026

- Added manual face marking, project protection, recent projects and a 64-bit
  Windows installer.

## 0.1.0 – 6 August 2026

- First installable test build with storyboard, XMP/digiKam import, global image
  alignment, quality indicators, filtering and thumbnail cache.
